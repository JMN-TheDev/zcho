01RESPECT THE WATER

The sea is beautiful.
And it deserves respect.

Whether you’re swimming, boating, or simply exploring the shore, the ocean can change quickly. A few simple habits help keep every visit a good one.

A few things to remember ↓
01 / BEFORE YOU GO

Check conditions

Look up local weather, tides, and surf conditions. If the forecast turns, make a plan to head in.

↗
02 / WHEN YOU ARRIVE

Know your way out

Notice signs, flags, currents, and safe entry points. Ask a lifeguard if something doesn’t look right.

↗
03 / STAY TOGETHER

Bring a buddy

Keep an eye on one another, stay within your comfort zone, and make sure someone knows your plan.

↗
04 / IF IN DOUBT

Choose caution

Don’t enter water that feels unsafe. If someone is in trouble, alert a lifeguard or emergency services.

↗

General awareness only. Follow local beach guidance, lifeguard instructions, and emergency advice.
